# Support HackMan3D Control Deck

HackMan3D Control Deck is designed, developed and shared free of charge.
Support helps pay for prototype electronics, test prints and the development
time required to maintain the desktop application and firmware.

## Ways to help

- [Make a one-time contribution with PayPal](https://paypal.me/Hackman3D)
- Star the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/HackMan3D/Hackman3D-Control-Deck)
- Share a photo or video of your build and tag HackMan3D
- Report issues and suggest improvements

Every contribution is optional. The application remains usable without a
payment, and no feature is locked behind a donation.

## What support funds

- HCD Plus prototype components and validation
- HCD Pro display, electronics and enclosure testing
- Windows, macOS and Linux compatibility work
- Firmware, documentation and assembly-guide improvements

The public roadmap shows only an overall percentage. Financial amounts and
supporter identities are not published by the application.
